⭐ Your own voice model

No model yet.

1 · Record sentences

Read each sentence naturally, in the voice you want for your videos. Quiet room, same spot, phone about 15–20 cm away. You can stop any time — progress is saved.

2 · Export

Packs your recordings into myvoice-dataset.zip in Downloads/MyVoice.

3 · Train on Google Colab (free)

  1. Upload myvoice-dataset.zip to Google Drive into a folder named MyVoice.
  2. Open the training notebook (works in your phone's browser too):
    Open in Colab
  3. Runtime → Change runtime type → T4 GPU, then Runtime → Run all. Takes about 1–3 hours. Keep the Colab tab open; if it disconnects, tap Run all again and it carries on from the last save.
  4. When done, my_voice.onnx and my_voice.onnx.json are in Drive → MyVoice. Tap Import my model above and pick both.

Your recordings and model stay on your phone and your own Google Drive.
